Privacy / prototype warning: Face descriptors are biometric data and are stored in the connected Supabase project. RLS limits records to the signed-in owner account, but this prototype loads that owner’s templates into the browser for matching. Do not use it for official payroll or multi-employee production attendance yet. A production shared kiosk should move template matching behind a secured backend, add role controls and audit logs, improve liveness detection, and implement the applicable privacy requirements.
