Each attempt chooses a random action, such as blinking once, blinking twice, or turning your head and returning to center.

No button is needed after the challenge. FaceCheck captures a set of face descriptors and matches them with a registered employee.

Time In and Time Out alternate after successful face verification. Blink and head-turn challenges provide basic liveness checks, not a guarantee against every spoofing method.
